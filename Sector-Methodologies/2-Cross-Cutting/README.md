# Cross-cutting methods

These apply across every sector rather than to one in particular.

| Topic | Organization | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| [Asset Ownership](./asset-ownership/) | Global Energy Monitor | Linking assets to the companies that own and operate them |
| [Non-Greenhouse Gases](./non-greenhouse-gases/) | The Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory; WattTime | Co-pollutants emitted alongside greenhouse gases, including PM2.5 population exposure |
| [Other Sectors](./other-sectors/) | WattTime | How subsectors without a dedicated model are estimated, through data-informed disaggregation and implicit estimation |
| [Spatial Disaggregation](./spatial-disaggregation/) | The Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory; WattTime | Distributing spatially uncertain, country-level emissions to finer geographic areas |
| [Temporal Disaggregation](./temporal-disaggregation/) | WattTime; The Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory | Converting annual and quarterly estimates into monthly emissions |
| [Completeness and Metadata](./completeness-metadata/) | WattTime; The Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory | Completeness of bottom-up emissions estimates and their associated metadata |
| [Emissions-Reducing Solutions](./emissions-reducing-solutions/) | WattTime | The framework for estimating emissions-reduction potential of solutions applied to assets |
| [Visualization of PM2.5 Plumes](./pm2.5-pollution/) | Carnegie Mellon University CREATE Lab | High resolution visualization of PM2.5 impacts on surrounding population |
