## Subsectors

Subsector names below are the same ones used in the [data downloads](https://climatetrace.org/data)
and the API. Each links to the methodology that documents it.

| Subsector | Description | Organization |
|---|---|---|
| [`forestry-and-land-use_forest-land-clearing`](«methodology.pdf») | Emissions from forest converted to other land uses | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_forest-land-degradation`](«methodology.pdf») | Emissions from forest that loses biomass without being cleared | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_forest-land-fires`](«methodology.pdf») | Emissions from fires burning in forested land | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_net-forest-land`](«methodology.pdf») | Net balance of emissions and removals on land remaining forest | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_net-shrubgrass`](«methodology.pdf») | Net balance of emissions and removals on shrubland and grassland | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_net-wetland`](«methodology.pdf») | Net balance of emissions and removals on wetlands | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_removals`](«methodology.pdf») | Carbon drawn out of the atmosphere and stored in vegetation and soil | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_shrubgrass-fires`](«methodology.pdf») | Emissions from fires on shrubland and grassland | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_water-reservoirs`](«methodology.pdf») | Methane and carbon dioxide from artificial reservoirs | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_wetland-fires`](«methodology.pdf») | Emissions from fires burning in wetlands, including peat | CTrees |

Subsectors marked *Other Sectors* have no dedicated model. They are estimated by
[data-informed disaggregation or implicit estimation](../../2-Cross-Cutting/other-sectors/).
