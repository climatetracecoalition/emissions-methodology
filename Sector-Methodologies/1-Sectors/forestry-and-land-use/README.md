## Subsectors

Subsector names below are the same ones used in the [data downloads](https://climatetrace.org/data)
and the API. Each links to the methodology that documents it.

| Subsector | Description | Organization |
|---|---|---|
| [`forestry-and-land-use_forest-land-clearing`](./forestry-carbon-stock-change-and-emissions/README.md) | Emissions from forest converted to other land uses | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_forest-land-degradation`](./forestry-carbon-stock-change-and-emissions/README.md) | Emissions from forest that loses biomass without being cleared | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_forest-land-fires`](./forestry-carbon-stock-change-and-emissions/README.md) | Emissions from fires burning in forested land | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_net-forest-land`](./forestry-carbon-stock-change-and-emissions/README.md) | Net balance of emissions and removals on land remaining forest | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_net-shrubgrass`](./forestry-carbon-stock-change-and-emissions/README.md) | Net balance of emissions and removals on shrubland and grassland | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_net-soil-organic-carbon`](./net-soil-organic-carbon/README.md) | Net change in carbon stored in agricultural soils | Michigan State University, Dept. of Earth and Environmental Sciences; Carbon Yield |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_net-wetland`](./forestry-carbon-stock-change-and-emissions/README.md) | Net balance of emissions and removals on wetlands | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_removals`](./forestry-carbon-stock-change-and-emissions/README.md) | Carbon drawn out of the atmosphere and stored in vegetation and soil | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_shrubgrass-fires`](./forestry-carbon-stock-change-and-emissions/README.md) | Emissions from fires on shrubland and grassland | CTrees |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_water-reservoirs`](./reservoirs/README.md) | Methane and carbon dioxide from artificial reservoirs | The Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory |
| [`forestry-and-land-use_wetland-fires`](./forestry-carbon-stock-change-and-emissions/README.md) | Emissions from fires burning in wetlands, including peat | CTrees |

Subsectors marked *Other Sectors* have no dedicated model. They are estimated by
[data-informed disaggregation or implicit estimation](../../2-Cross-Cutting/other-sectors/).
