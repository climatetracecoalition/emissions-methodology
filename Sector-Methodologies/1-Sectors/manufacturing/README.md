## Subsectors

Subsector names below are the same ones used in the [data downloads](https://climatetrace.org/data)
and the API. Each links to the methodology that documents it.

| Subsector | Description | Organization |
|---|---|---|
| [`manufacturing_aluminum`](«methodology.pdf») | Smelting and refining of aluminum | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_cement`](«methodology.pdf») | Clinker production, including CO2 released chemically from limestone | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_chemicals`](«methodology.pdf») | Production of industrial chemicals | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_food-beverage-tobacco`](«methodology.pdf») | Processing of food, drink and tobacco products | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_glass`](«methodology.pdf») | Melting and forming of glass | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_iron-and-steel`](«methodology.pdf») | Blast furnaces, electric arc furnaces and steelmaking | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_lime`](«methodology.pdf») | Calcination of limestone to produce lime | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_other-chemicals`](«methodology.pdf») | Chemical production not covered by a dedicated category | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_other-manufacturing`](«methodology.pdf») | Remaining industrial production | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_other-metals`](«methodology.pdf») | Production of metals other than iron, steel and aluminum | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_petrochemical-steam-cracking`](«methodology.pdf») | Cracking of hydrocarbons to produce ethylene and related products | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_pulp-and-paper`](«methodology.pdf») | Pulping, paper making and the heat used in both | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_textiles-leather-apparel`](«methodology.pdf») | Textile, leather and garment production | «organization» |
| [`manufacturing_wood-and-wood-products`](«methodology.pdf») | Sawmilling and wood product manufacturing | «organization» |

Subsectors marked *Other Sectors* have no dedicated model. They are estimated by
[data-informed disaggregation or implicit estimation](../../2-Cross-Cutting/other-sectors/).
