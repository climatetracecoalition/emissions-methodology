## Subsectors

Subsector names below are the same ones used in the [data downloads](https://climatetrace.org/data)
and the API. Each links to the methodology that documents it.

| Subsector | Description | Organization |
|---|---|---|
| [`waste_biological-treatment-of-solid-waste-and-biogenic`](../../2-Cross-Cutting/other-sectors/README.md) | Composting and anaerobic digestion of organic waste | WattTime |
| [`waste_domestic-wastewater-treatment-and-discharge`](./wastewater-treatment-plants/README.md) | Treatment and discharge of household sewage | The Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory |
| [`waste_incineration-and-open-burning-of-waste`](../../2-Cross-Cutting/other-sectors/README.md) | Waste burned in facilities or in the open | WattTime |
| [`waste_industrial-wastewater-treatment-and-discharge`](./wastewater-treatment-plants/README.md) | Treatment and discharge of industrial effluent | The Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory |
| [`waste_solid-waste-disposal`](./solid-waste-disposal/README.md) | Methane from landfills and dumps | RMI |

Subsectors marked *Other Sectors* have no dedicated model. They are estimated by
[data-informed disaggregation or implicit estimation](../../2-Cross-Cutting/other-sectors/).
